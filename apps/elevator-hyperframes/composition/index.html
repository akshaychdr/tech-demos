Traction elevator

Machine room → ropes → car & counterweight

Traction elevators use steel ropes over a motor-driven sheave.
Machine room: AC motor turns the sheave; the brake holds the sheave when stopped.
Hoist ropes tie the car and counterweight — they move in opposite directions. Counterweight ≈ empty car + 40–50% of rated load (not live passenger weight).
Guide rails keep the car and counterweight aligned in the shaft.
Normal run: motor lifts the car at rated speed. —
Overspeed: governor trips; safety gear wedges clamp the guide rails. Illustrative free-fall scenario — real systems include buffers and multiple safeties.
